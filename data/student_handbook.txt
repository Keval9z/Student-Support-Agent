STUDENT SUPPORT HANDBOOK
Academic Demonstration Policy

SECTION 1: FEE PAYMENT POLICIES
Students can check their current fee status and balance through the support portal.
If an online payment portal transaction fails, students must retain the transaction reference number and receipt screenshot.
Students should email the accounts desk at accounts@institute.edu within 24 hours of a failed debit.
A failed transaction debit does not grant an automatic fee deadline extension or late penalty waiver.
Payment deadlines are published on the academic calendar and require formal clearance before semester examinations.

SECTION 2: SCHOLARSHIP POLICIES
Scholarship applications carry one of four explicit statuses: Approved, Rejected, Pending, or Under Review.
A status of Under Review indicates committee evaluation is active; it does not confer financial clearance.
Tuition balance calculations remain unadjusted until a scholarship status updates to Approved.
Final scholarship determinations are made exclusively by the Financial Aid Committee.

SECTION 3: PROGRAMME AND ELECTIVE CHANGES
Students requesting an elective or programme transfer must submit a formal change request.
Academic record changes are sensitive actions and require verification from the Academic Office.
A programme change request remains in Pending status until authorized administrative staff reviews and approves it.
If an administrator rejects the change, the student's existing enrolled programme remains strictly unchanged.

SECTION 4: SUPPORT DESK AND PRIVACY RULES
Students may open general support tickets regarding fee discrepancies, portal issues, or course access.
Students must never enter passwords, one-time passwords (OTPs), or recovery phrases into support tickets or assistant chat.
Students are strictly permitted to inspect only their personal academic and financial records.
Direct inspection or manipulation of another student's educational record is prohibited by institutional policy.
